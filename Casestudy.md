**MyWealthLens – Case Study**

**Problem**

Most Indian investors track their money across multiple apps and spreadsheets — mutual funds, stocks, insurance, and family assets are scattered. There is no simple, unified tool that gives a complete picture of personal and family wealth.
Approach

I decided to build a single platform focused on the Indian financial ecosystem. The goal was to combine portfolio tracking (with CAS & XIRR support), goal planning, family wealth mapping, insurance, and retirement planning in one place, using Python and Flask.
What I Built

----

**MyWealthLens currently includes:**

Wealth Centre with mutual fund (CAS + XIRR) and stock tracking
Goal planning with glide-path projections
Family Centre with member and heir management
Insurance and Retirement modules
User authentication and basic dashboard

The project is live at: **https://mywealthlens.onrender.com**

----

**Challenges**

Handling Indian-specific formats (CAS statements and XIRR calculations)
Structuring multiple modules while keeping the codebase manageable
Deploying a Flask application correctly on a cloud platform
Balancing features with limited development experience

----

**Learnings**

Domain knowledge (Finance MBA) is a strong advantage when building fintech tools
Starting with a clear structure early saves time later
Shipping a working version (even if incomplete) is more valuable than waiting for perfection
Real deployment teaches more than local development alone
