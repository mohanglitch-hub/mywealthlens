Not a filing document. This shows the gross foreign dividend and tax withheld figures you've recorded against each holding — the two INPUT figures Form 67 asks for. It does not compute the actual DTAA foreign tax credit, which is capped at the lower of tax paid abroad and the Indian tax payable on that same income, and depends on your full tax computation (slab, other income, deductions) that MyWealthLens has no visibility into. Confirm the creditable amount and file Form 67 with a CA before relying on this for a return.

Each dividend is converted at the SBI TT buying rate for the last day of the previous month (the Rule 115 convention for income), from a rate typed on that dividend, else your rate book. The rupee total for a holding is left blank if any one of its dividends has no rate. Native-currency figures are always shown.
