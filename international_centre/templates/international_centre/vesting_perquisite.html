Perquisite value is (FMV at vest − price actually paid) × quantity, taxable as SALARY income in the FY the shares vested — a separate, earlier event from any capital gain when those shares are later sold (see the Capital Gains report, which uses this same FMV as the cost basis). Your employer's actual reported figure (Form 12BA/Form 16) may value FMV differently — always confirm with your employer's records and a CA before filing.

The rupee figure uses the SBI TT buying rate on the last day of the month before the vest month (the Rule 115 convention for salary), taken from a rate typed on the tranche, then your rate book. Your employer's Form 12BA figure is the one that counts for filing.
