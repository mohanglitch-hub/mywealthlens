Conversion conventions
The form's instructions say the rate on the date of each event. The closing value always uses 31 December itself.
Practitioners differ on this. It is one of the questions in the CA brief.

Dividends, Form 67 and the RSU perquisite always use the last day of the previous month, the Rule 115 convention for income and salary.
