Increases your monthly SIP by this % every year (e.g. with salary hikes). Leave 0 for a flat SIP.
💡 What inflation rate means
At 6% inflation, ₹1 Cr target today becomes ₹1.8 Cr in 10 years. Your SIP is recalculated against the inflation-adjusted target.
💡 What SIP step-up means
A ₹10,000/mo SIP with a 10% yearly step-up becomes ₹11,000/mo in year 2, ₹12,100/mo in year 3, and so on — reaching your target faster without one big jump.
Sets the glide path — how your equity/debt mix should shift as the goal nears. Same value for both = no shift.
